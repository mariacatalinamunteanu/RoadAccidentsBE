***** Include this explanation in the question mark dot that follows "How is this answer computed?" in the tab Density vs Severity: "Spearman ρ (“rho”) compares two rankings. Put the municipalities in order by population density, then put them in order by the share of injury crashes that were severe. If denser municipalities tend to rank lower on that second list, ρ is negative. It ranges from −1 (exactly opposite rankings) to +1 (identical rankings). ρ = −0.50 shows a clear tendency for denser municipalities to have a lower severe share.
The p-value asks: if there were no association, how often would we see a correlation this strong or stronger in either direction? p < 0.001 means less than 1 time in 1,000 under that assumption. That’s strong evidence of an association. Whether density causes the difference is a separate question." Use a writing style that is easy to read, wiht tabs and bullet points, etc
